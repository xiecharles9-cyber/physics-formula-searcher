Magnetic Force on a Wire (F = BIL)

F = B·I·L

What it means

The force on a current-carrying wire in a magnetic field, depending on field strength, current, and wire length.

Variables

SymbolQuantitySI unit
FforceN
Bmagnetic fieldT
IcurrentA
Llengthm

Worked example

Given B = 0.5 T, I = 2 A, L = 3 m, solve for F:

F = 3 N
