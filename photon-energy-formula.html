Photon Energy Formula (E = hf)

E = h·f

What it means

The energy of a single photon is Planck's constant times its frequency — a cornerstone of quantum physics.

Worked example

Given h = 6.6260e-34 J·s, f = 5.0000e+14 Hz, solve for E:

E = 3.3130e-19 J
